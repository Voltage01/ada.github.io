About

Technical mind, artistic instinct.

My work naturally moves between engineering and art. On one side, I care about circuits, systems, control, embedded logic, and the physical behavior behind technology. On the other, I care about music, sonic identity, aesthetics, and how a project feels when someone actually experiences it.

I’m especially drawn to projects involving instrument building, sound design, real-time interaction, physics-inspired problem solving, and research directions like quantum technologies.
