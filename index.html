About

NOT JUST TECH

My work sits between engineering and artistic expression. I care about circuits, systems, control, embedded logic, and physical behavior — but I also care about mood, texture, rhythm, sound, visuals, and how a thing actually feels when someone encounters it.

I’m most drawn to instrument building, real-time interaction, sound-centered design, audiovisual performance ideas, and research directions where physics opens new creative possibilities.
